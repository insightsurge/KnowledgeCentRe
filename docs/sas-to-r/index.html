SAS to R: The Migration Guide

A comprehensive curriculum for clinical programmers transitioning from SAS to R.

Welcome, SAS User.

You’ve spent years mastering the Data Step, PROC SQL, and the art of the macro language. You think in SET, MERGE, and BY statements. Now, you’re looking at R, and it feels… different.

You are in the right place.

This guide isn’t just about syntax; it’s about translating your mental models. We map your existing SAS knowledge directly to R equivalents, specifically focusing on the clinical trial domain.

Curriculum

1. Introduction: SAS vs R

Stop thinking in iterations. Start thinking in vectors and functions.

2. The R Ecosystem & Tools

Installation, IDEs, and setting up your first project.

3. Data Structures: Vectors & Frames

Vectors, Lists, and Data Frames: The building blocks of R.

4. Data Import & Export

Where is my LIBNAME? Reading and writing data with haven and readr.

5. Data Manipulation (dplyr)

The DATA step and PROC SQL reborn in dplyr.

6. Merging & Joining Data

Mastering left_join, inner_join, and anti_join.

7. Advanced Programming: Functions

Replacing SAS Macros with R Functions and iteration.

8. Tables & Reporting

Replacements for PROC REPORT and PROC TABULATE using gt and tfrmt.

9. Clinical Workflows (CDISC)

CDISC implementation: Creating ADaM and SDTM datasets in R.

10. Package Development

Building robust, reproducible tools for your team.
